Built 2026-08-19 before the corporate file was uploaded to AX, but one day
after the forecast origin. This is a reproducible as-of reconstruction and an
operational shadow, not a clean prospective contestant.

## Decision

- Keep the corporate forecast as the AX upload for this horizon. No challenger
  was frozen before August 18, so this pack does not authorize replacing the
  production upload.
- The best research challenger is now clearly
  `catpool_corporate_anchor_activation`. It has the lowest SKU WAPE in all
  three completed corporate-vintage reconstructions. Keep it as the primary
  shadow and stop treating the no-activation category arm or global
  recent-shape arm as equally likely winners.
- Do not wait passively until August 31. When the next corporate file becomes
  available, run the same freeze before its `ForecastStartDate`. That next
  clean-origin pack is the prospective confirmation needed for a production
  promotion decision.
- Close this August 18-31 pack on September 1 or as soon as all 14 monitoring
  days are complete. If a weekly corporate overlay is uploaded during the
  horizon, preserve and score it separately as an operational vintage.

## Three Completed Corporate Vintages

The July 7, July 21, and August 4 corporate starts were compared with the same
four corporate-total arms. Category candidates reconstructed after their
origins remain diagnostics; this table does not relabel them as prospective.

| Origin | Corporate raw WAPE | Recent shape WAPE | Category, no activation WAPE | Category + activation WAPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-07-07 | 1.548 | 1.051 | 1.065 | **0.890** |
| 2026-07-21 | 1.291 | 1.154 | 1.149 | **0.981** |
| 2026-08-04 | 1.194 | 1.072 | 1.072 | **0.939** |
| Simple mean | 1.344 | 1.093 | 1.095 | **0.937** |

The activation challenger wins all three windows. Relative to corporate raw,
its mean WAPE is lower by 0.407 absolute, and mean sold-unit coverage rises
from 47.56% to 74.62%. The tradeoff is visible rather than hidden:

- mean forecast-SKU use rate falls from 85.56% to 82.58%;
- mean zero-demand forecast-unit share rises from 9.57% to 10.60%;
- the no-activation category arm does not beat global recent shape on average,
  so activation—not category reconciliation alone—is the demonstrated source
  of the gain.

The compact matrix and provenance are under:

```text

```

The July 7 and July 21 reconstructions use a July 22 current-value category
crosswalk rather than an as-of/SCD category history. The August 4 reconstruction
uses pre-origin but stale July 22 inventory/inbound. These are genuine
point-in-time limitations, which is why the result selects the primary
challenger but does not by itself promote it into AX.
